# 🎓 Online Courses

> Multi-page educational platform for online learning and professional development.

---

## ✨ About

Online Courses is a modern multi-page website designed for online education, skill development, and digital learning.

The platform allows students to explore courses, learn from professional instructors, and improve their knowledge through interactive online lessons.

---

- 💻 Programming & Development
- 🎨 UI/UX Design
- 📈 Marketing
- 🌐 Foreign Languages
- 📊 Data Analytics
- 🧠 Personal Development

- 📱 Responsive modern design
- 🎥 Online video lessons
- 👨‍🏫 Instructor profiles
- 📄 Course detail pages

- 🌐 Multi-page navigation
